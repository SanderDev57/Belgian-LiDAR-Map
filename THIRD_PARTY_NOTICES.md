## Services deliberately excluded
No calls are made to Esri World Imagery, CARTO, public Nominatim, or a third-party library CDN.
Place and address search uses https://photon.komoot.io/api/ only after Search or Enter. Photon / komoot receives the submitted query and IP/browser information. Numeric coordinates are parsed locally. Search data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Photon service information: https://github.com/komoot/photon; provider privacy: https://www.komoot.com/privacy. The public demo has limited capacity and no availability guarantee; only one user-triggered search runs at a time, without autocomplete or bulk requests.

## Hosting
No hosting account, analytics, cookie system or deployment-specific privacy controller details are configured here. Public deployers must provide their own operator/contact/host logging information.
A public repository does not grant permission to overload providers, remove credits, resell imagery or ignore future service changes.

- French imagery and street map: IGN / Géoplateforme BD ORTHO (typically 20 cm, dates vary), https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/bd-ortho-r ; Plan IGN, https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/plan-ign . All these French datasets use Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0: https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/ . Source consulted 10 November 2026; capture/update dates are area-specific in linked metadata. Display changes include reprojection, clipping, scaling, layer combination and opacity. Coverage here is metropolitan France including Corsica, not overseas territories. HD gaps, protected-area exclusions and unissued tiles expose the available fallback.
- IGN service: cached public GeoPlateforme WMTS https://data.geopf.fr/wmts . Conditions: https://cartes.gouv.fr/cgu/ ; current limits: https://cartes.gouv.fr/aide/fr/guides-utilisateur/utiliser-les-services-de-la-geoplateforme/limites-d-usage/ . Provider privacy: https://cartes.gouv.fr/donnees-personnelles/ ; https://www.ign.fr/institut/donnees-caractere-personnel . No access key is embedded.
- Natural Earth admin-0/admin-1 country and region polygons, 1:10 million, public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ ; source https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson and https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson . Filtered to European Netherlands and metropolitan France, with Belgium, Germany and connected regional footprints for request filtering, coordinates rounded for display. Generalized masks prevent opaque aerial no-data outside each country from hiding neighbouring sources; they can produce narrow border/coastal gaps and must not be treated as survey boundaries. These small boundary extracts are included in `js/coverage.js`; elevation/imagery databases are not included.

Only visible-area sources are attached. AHN requests share a queue of at most three active images and eight starts per second; stale requests are cancelled. IGN requests share at most six active fetches, with cancellation and a 30-second timeout. Missing IGN cached tiles (404) are transparent no-data; other errors receive at most one delayed retry. No prefetch, bulk download, offline pack or raw imagery rehosting. Map attribution and privacy information identify the new providers before map requests start.
