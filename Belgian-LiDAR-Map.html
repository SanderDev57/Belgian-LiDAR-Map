Belgian LiDAR Map

Explore Belgian, German, Dutch, French and Luxembourg terrain with automatic detail and satellite / aerial comparison.

Opening the map loads street maps from OpenStreetMap Belgium / Champs-Libres and, where visible, BKG, PDOK, IGN or Luxembourg ACT. Enabling terrain or aerial imagery automatically contacts the sources covering the viewed area: Digitaal Vlaanderen, SPW, BKG, Geobasis NRW, LVermGeo Rheinland-Pfalz, AHN via Ellipsis Drive, PDOK, IGN / Géoplateforme, Luxembourg ACT / Geoportail.lu and EOX. These providers receive your IP address and the area you view. Place and address searches connect to Photon / komoot when submitted.

Provider privacy information

This viewer requests maps directly from the providers you choose. They receive your IP address, browser/request information and tile coordinates, which reveal the area being viewed. Place and address searches are sent to Photon / komoot only when you press Search or Enter. This service receives your search text, IP address and browser/request information. Coordinate input is processed locally. The application adds no analytics, cookies or browser storage; the current map position is kept in the URL fragment and can be included when you share the link.

Digitaal Vlaanderen: the agency processes request logs and IP addresses to secure its geographic services as a task in the public interest. IP addresses are retained for at most 90 days and are accessible to service administrators. You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or object to processing; objecting may prevent service access. Contact privacy@digitaal.vlaanderen.be. See its full service privacy notice, controller details and complaint information.

German services: automatic terrain, street maps and aerial imagery send relevant map requests directly to BKG, Geobasis NRW or LVermGeo Rheinland-Pfalz. See BKG privacy, Bezirksregierung Köln privacy and LVermGeoRP privacy.

Netherlands: AHN terrain requests go to Ellipsis Drive; aerial and street requests go to PDOK. These services receive your IP/browser information and viewed tiles. See Ellipsis Drive privacy and PDOK privacy.

France: map, imagery and terrain requests go to IGN / Géoplateforme. See cartes.gouv.fr privacy and IGN personal-data information.

Luxembourg: terrain requests go to the public ACT WMS, and aerial/basemap requests go to the ACT open-data WMTS. They receive your IP/browser information and viewed tiles. ACT / Geoportail.lu terms and privacy information.

EOX: satellite overview requests go to EOX IT Services GmbH, which receives your IP/browser information and viewed tiles. EOX privacy. The overview is the CC BY 4.0 2016 layer (2016–17 imagery), not current aerial photography.

SPW: consult the SPW privacy notice; privacy contact: dpo@spw.wallonie.be.

OpenStreetMap Belgium / Champs-Libres: street-map requests go to their tile server. See the service and attribution policy and tile provider.

When this viewer is hosted online, the website host also receives ordinary page requests and may keep access logs. Hosting-specific privacy information must be supplied by the website operator.

No map requests are sent until you open the map. You can leave this page without loading it.

Map access is subject to the Digitaal Vlaanderen service conditions, SPW service conditions and, when selected, the OSM Belgium tile policy.

Sources, licences & privacy

Independent viewer; no provider affiliation or endorsement. Source permissions were reviewed on 10 November 2026 for this public interactive viewer. Map data keep their own licences and service conditions; the MIT code licence does not cover them. Keep provider credits and licence links visible, including in shared screenshots. Source resolution, capture date and positional accuracy differ; this is not a survey or a guarantee that features exist. A disclaimer does not grant extra data rights.

  • Flanders terrain: DHM II hillshade. Bron: Digitaal Vlaanderen. Public geographic service use is subject to the linked dataset and service conditions.
  • Flanders/Brussels aerial imagery: most recent winter orthophoto mosaic. Bron: Luchtopnamen Digitaal Vlaanderen. See dataset metadata for dates and reuse conditions.
  • Wallonia terrain: Service public de Wallonie (SPW), MNT 2021–2022 hillshade, © SPW 2021–2022, CC BY 4.0.
  • Wallonia aerial imagery: Service public de Wallonie (SPW), Orthophotos 2023 Été, © SPW 2023, CC BY 4.0. This full-coverage 2023 layer is not a claim to the latest imagery everywhere.
  • SPW services: service terms. Normal interactive viewing only; no bulk downloads or prefetching.
  • Germany overview and colour map: © GeoBasis-DE / BKG (2026), CC BY 4.0. Terrain documentation and terms. Hillshade uses DGM5 with rendered pixels approximately 6.4 m; the viewer automatically overlays the connected finer regional layers.
  • North Rhine-Westphalia: Geobasis NRW · 0.25 m hillshade and 10 cm aerial photography, dl-de/zero-2-0.
  • Rhineland-Palatinate: ©GeoBasis-DE / LVermGeoRP 2026, dl-de/by-2-0 [Daten bearbeitet]. Hillshade 1 m metadata and 20 cm aerial photography.
  • Netherlands terrain: Actueel Hoogtebestand Nederland (AHN), 0.5 m ground model, AHN4 (CC0), AHN5/6 (CC BY 4.0), public services published through Ellipsis Drive. Newer available pixels overlay older coverage. The viewer requests a modified hillshade rendering; real no-data gaps remain.
  • Netherlands aerial imagery: Samenwerkingsverband Beeldmateriaal / PDOK, current high-resolution mosaic, 2026 release, 5–8 cm; CC BY 4.0. Street map: Kadaster / PDOK BRT Achtergrondkaart, CC BY 4.0. PDOK conditions.
  • Metropolitan France and Corsica: © IGN / Géoplateforme, LiDAR HD MNT 50 cm, with RGE ALTI / broader terrain underneath; BD ORTHO aerial imagery (typically 20 cm) and Plan IGN. Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0. Sources consulted 10 November 2026; capture/update dates vary by area and are given in the linked source metadata. HD coverage is being published progressively; some protected areas and no-data gaps are absent. Service terms.
  • Luxembourg: Administration du cadastre et de la topographie (ACT) / Geoportail.lu, LiDAR 2024 MNT hillshade (50 cm source model), 2025 summer aerial imagery and Carte de base, CC0. Public WMS/WMTS; source rendering and generalised country-edge masks are retained. This does not claim access to restricted layers.
  • Country/region display masks: Natural Earth, public domain, generalized 1:10 million boundaries. Used to clip Dutch/French imagery and street maps; narrow edge gaps are possible. These are not survey boundaries.
  • Satellite fallback: EOxCloudless by EOX IT Services GmbH (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016 & 2017), CC BY 4.0. Product licence and attribution. Only the 2016 vintage is used; later vintages have different conditions. This older overview fills gaps outside the connected detailed aerial services.
  • Street map: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL data), tiles generated by Champs-Libres, under the Belgian tile-service policy.

Display changes: services reproject imagery for this map; the viewer clips, scales and changes opacity to compare layers. The composite puts finer sources above coarser ones, with transparent gaps showing the underlying source. AHN elevations are rendered as grayscale hillshade (315° illumination, 45° altitude, no vertical exaggeration). It does not edit the underlying source datasets. Higher zoom does not add native detail. Keep credits visible in shared screenshots.
